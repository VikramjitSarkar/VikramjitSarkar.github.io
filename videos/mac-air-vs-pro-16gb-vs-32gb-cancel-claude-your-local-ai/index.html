Should you upgrade your 16GB M1 for local AI? MacBook Air or MacBook Pro? And can a local LLM actually replace your Claude subscription when you hit the rate limit?

Key numbers from this test

16GB M1 for vibe coding? It's still a beast — you do NOT need to upgrade. I built full mobile + web apps with backend on an 8GB M1 before moving to the M5.
Upgrading anyway for content creation + local LLMs? Skip 32GB — go 64GB, because 32GB hits the RAM wall fast.
Hit your Claude Pro rate limit — switch to local LLM? Honest answer: no. $20/month ($240/year) vs a $2,400 MacBook is 10x the cost, and I've tested the local models — they're not at cloud level for serious coding. Stick to your subscription (or upgrade the plan).
Want local anyway? You'd need a 128GB MacBook Max (~$4,000) to get anywhere close — and even Llama 70B isn't matching Claude Sonnet.
